Help Center

Everything you need to get started and make the most of your cloud desktop.

Getting Started

Connect to your desktop for the first time

Sign up and choose your plan

Go to the Pricing page, select your region and configuration, enter your name and email, then click "Claim My Free 30-Day Trial". You'll receive a confirmation email within a few minutes.

Check your email for credentials

Within 15 minutes, you'll receive an email with your WorkSpaces registration code, username, and a link to set your password. Keep this email — you'll need the registration code every time you connect.

Download the client or use your browser

Visit the Downloads page to get the app for your device. Or go directly to the web client — no installation needed.

Enter your registration code and log in

Open the WorkSpaces client. Enter your registration code from the email. Then enter your username and password. Your Windows desktop will load within seconds. You're in!

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a cloud desktop? ▼
A cloud desktop is a full Windows computer that runs on servers in an Amazon data center instead of on your local hardware. You connect to it over the internet and see your desktop just like a normal PC — but it's actually running remotely. This means you can access it from any device, anywhere.
Do I need a credit card to start the free trial? ▼
No credit card is required to start your 30-day free trial. We do require basic identity verification to prevent abuse and ensure the service remains available for genuine users. You will only be asked for payment information when you choose to continue after the trial period.
What happens when my 30-day trial ends? ▼
On Day 25 of your trial, we'll send you an email reminder. If you choose to continue, your workspace will automatically convert to a paid monthly subscription at the price shown on your plan. If you don't want to continue, simply cancel before Day 30 and you won't be charged anything.
Can I access my workspace from multiple devices? ▼
Yes! You can access your workspace from Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android, and any modern web browser. Your desktop is the same regardless of which device you use — all your files and settings are stored in the cloud, not on your device.
Is my data safe? What if I lose my device? ▼
Your data is completely safe. Everything is stored in Amazon's encrypted data centers — nothing is saved on your local device. If you lose your phone or laptop, your workspace and all your data remain secure in the cloud. Simply log in from a new device.
Which region should I choose? ▼
Choose the region closest to your physical location for the best performance and lowest latency. For example, if you're in Europe, choose Ireland, Frankfurt, London, or Paris. If you need a specific regional IP address for business purposes (e.g., accessing US-only services), choose the corresponding US region.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan? ▼
Yes, you can change your plan at any time through your dashboard. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. Contact our support team if you need assistance with plan changes.
What software comes pre-installed on my workspace? ▼
Your workspace comes with Windows 10 or 11, Microsoft Office applications, and standard productivity tools. You can install any additional software you need, just like on a regular Windows PC. Your installations persist between sessions.
How do I cancel my subscription? ▼
You can cancel anytime from your dashboard under "Account Settings". Cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period. You'll retain access to your workspace until the period ends. After cancellation, your workspace data is securely deleted within 30 days.
What internet speed do I need? ▼
For a smooth experience, we recommend at least 10 Mbps download speed. For video calls or high-resolution work, 25 Mbps or more is ideal. The WorkSpaces client automatically adjusts quality based on your connection speed.

Still have questions?

Our support team is here to help. Submit a ticket from your dashboard or reach out directly.

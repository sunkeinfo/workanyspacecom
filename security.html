Security & Technology

Your data stays in the cloud.
Never on your device.

WorkAnySpace is built on Amazon Web Services — the world's most comprehensive and broadly adopted cloud platform, trusted by millions of businesses globally.

AWS Global Infrastructure

Your desktop runs inside Amazon's world-class data centers across 15 regions. AWS maintains 99.9%+ uptime SLAs, redundant power, and physical security that exceeds any on-premise solution.

End-to-End Encryption

All data in transit is encrypted using PCoIP or WSP protocols with AES-256 encryption. Data at rest on your workspace volume is encrypted using AWS KMS-managed keys.

Multi-Factor Authentication

Enable MFA on your account for an additional layer of security. Even if your password is compromised, your workspace remains protected by a second verification step.

Zero Local Data Storage

Nothing is stored on your local device. When you disconnect, your session ends cleanly. Your files, applications, and data remain exclusively inside the AWS data center.

Network Isolation

Each workspace runs in an isolated Amazon VPC (Virtual Private Cloud). Your desktop is logically separated from other users, with dedicated network resources.

Compliance Ready

AWS infrastructure is certified for SOC 1/2/3, ISO 27001, PCI DSS, HIPAA, and more. WorkAnySpace inherits these compliance certifications for your peace of mind.

Activate with a gift card

  1. Click Buy an Amazon eGift Card and select the Email delivery option.
  2. Enter your plan amount (e.g. $25.00) and set the recipient email to your own address, then complete the purchase.
  3. Open your inbox and copy the claim code — 14–15 characters, in the form AQXX-XXXXXX-XXXX.
  4. Paste the claim code below and click Redeem & Activate Now.
